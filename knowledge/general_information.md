# Smart India Hackathon (SIH) - General Information & Overview

## 1. About Smart India Hackathon
Smart India Hackathon (SIH) is a nationwide initiative by the Ministry of Education's Innovation Cell (MIC) and AICTE to provide students with a platform to solve pressing challenges faced by government ministries, departments, public sector enterprises, and private organizations.

## 2. Hackathon Editions
- **SIH Junior**: For school students (Classes 6th to 12th).
- **SIH Senior (Higher Education)**: For undergraduate and postgraduate students from AICTE/UGC approved institutions across India.
- **Tracks**:
  - Software Edition (36 hours non-stop coding hackathon)
  - Hardware Edition (5 days non-stop product development hackathon)

## 3. General Rules & Disclaimers
- Official dates, notifications, and portal access must be verified on the official portal (`https://www.sih.gov.in`).
- Deadlines for college internal nomination, idea upload, and results are published on the official portal schedule.
- Intellectual Property (IP): The IP rights of the developed solution generally remain with the student team or institution unless specified otherwise by the sponsoring ministry/organization.
