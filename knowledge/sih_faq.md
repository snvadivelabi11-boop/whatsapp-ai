# Smart India Hackathon (SIH) - Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1: Can students from different colleges form a team?
**A**: No. All 6 members of a team must be from the same college/institute. Cross-college teams are not permitted under SIH guidelines.

### Q2: Is a female member mandatory in every team?
**A**: Yes. Each 6-member team must include at least one female student. Teams without at least one female member will be disqualified.

### Q3: Can a team submit ideas for multiple problem statements?
**A**: A single team can submit only one idea for one problem statement per category (Software / Hardware). However, a college can nominate multiple teams across different problem statements.

### Q4: Can a 1st year student participate in SIH?
**A**: Yes. Any regular undergraduate or postgraduate student enrolled in a recognized institution with a valid student ID is eligible.

### Q5: How many mentors can a team have?
**A**: A team can have up to 2 mentors. Mentors can be faculty members from the college or industry experts. Mentors are optional during initial submission but crucial for the Grand Finale.

### Q6: What is the prize money for SIH?
**A**: For each winning team per problem statement in the Grand Finale, the standard prize is usually INR 1,00,000 (One Lakh Rupees) awarded by the respective ministry/organization. If there is a tie, the prize may be shared as per jury discretion.

### Q7: Are travel and accommodation provided for the Grand Finale?
**A**: Designated nodal centers provide basic accommodation and meals during the 36-hour hackathon. Reimbursement or travel allowances depend on the specific guidelines issued by AICTE/MIC for that edition.

### Q8: What if our team member falls sick or cannot attend the finale?
**A**: Team composition changes are strictly restricted after initial portal nomination. In exceptional circumstances, substitutions require formal approval and verification from the College Principal / SPOC through the SIH support desk.

### Q9: Who can we contact for official confirmation or special requests?
**A**: For human assistance, you can request an Admin Escalation or request a direct callback from your college SIH SPOC / Helpdesk admin right here on this WhatsApp portal.
