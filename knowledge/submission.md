# Smart India Hackathon (SIH) - Idea Submission & Presentation Guidelines

## 1. Idea Submission Format
All shortlisted college teams must prepare and upload an Idea Presentation (PPT) following the official prescribed SIH format:

### Required PPT Template Slides (Max 5-7 slides):
1. **Slide 1: Cover Slide**
   - Problem Statement ID & Title
   - Team Name & College / Institute Name
   - Team Leader Name & Contact Details
   - Team Member Names & Genders
2. **Slide 2: Proposed Solution & Approach**
   - Clear description of the proposed solution.
   - Core value proposition and uniqueness.
   - Flowcharts / block diagrams explaining system workflow.
3. **Slide 3: Technical Architecture & Tech Stack**
   - Frontend, Backend, Database, Cloud, Hardware components.
   - AI/ML models, IoT protocols, or specific tools utilized.
   - APIs and external integrations.
4. **Slide 4: Feasibility & Viability**
   - Technical feasibility of developing the MVP.
   - Cost-effectiveness and deployment scalability.
   - Potential risks and mitigation strategies.
5. **Slide 5: Impact, Beneficiaries & Novelty**
   - Who will benefit from this solution (Target Ministry, Citizens, Industry).
   - How it solves the problem better than existing manual or legacy systems.
6. **Slide 6: Business Potential & Future Roadmap (Optional / Recommended)**
   - Potential for commercialization or direct government adoption.
   - Future enhancements post-hackathon.

## 2. File Format & Size Limits
- Format: PDF or PPTX (PDF is highly recommended to preserve styling).
- Maximum File Size: Usually limited to 10 MB on the SIH portal.
- Naming convention: `PSID_TeamName_CollegeName.pdf`

## 3. Evaluation & Judging Criteria
Submissions are evaluated on a 100-point scale:
- **Novelty and Innovation**: 25%
- **Feasibility and Practicality**: 20%
- **Technical Architecture & Complexity**: 20%
- **Impact and Scalability**: 15%
- **Clarity of Presentation & Completeness**: 20%

## 4. Important Tips for High Scores
- Keep slide text concise; use clear bullet points, flowcharts, and UI mockups.
- Avoid generic buzzwords; explain concrete algorithms or hardware components.
- Ensure the problem statement ID and title precisely match the official portal listing.
