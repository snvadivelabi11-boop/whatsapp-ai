# Smart India Hackathon (SIH) - Rules & Team Guidelines

## 1. Team Composition & Eligibility
- **Team Size**: Each team must consist of exactly **6 members** (including the Team Leader).
- **Mandatory Female Member**: At least **one female member** is mandatory in every team to encourage diversity.
- **College Representation**: All 6 members of a team must belong to the **same institute/college**. Inter-college teams are strictly not permitted.
- **Academic Eligibility**: All members must be regular, enrolled undergraduate or postgraduate students (B.Tech, B.E., B.Sc, BCA, MCA, M.Tech, etc.) with valid college ID cards.
- **Mentors**: Teams can have a maximum of **2 mentors**. Mentors can be industry professionals or college faculty members. (Mentors are optional during initial idea submission, but recommended for Grand Finale).

## 2. Selection Process & Rounds
1. **College Internal Hackathon**: Every participating institute must conduct an internal hackathon to shortlist the top teams before official portal nomination.
2. **Idea Submission**: Shortlisted teams submit their proposed solutions on the official SIH portal against chosen Problem Statements.
3. **Screening & Evaluation**: Industry and ministry evaluators review submitted idea presentations (PPTs).
4. **Grand Finale**: Shortlisted teams are invited to designated nodal centers across India for a continuous 36-hour physical/virtual hackathon.

## 3. Problem Statements
- Problem statements are released under two categories:
  1. **Software Edition**: Focuses on web apps, mobile apps, AI/ML, cloud, blockchain, cybersecurity, etc.
  2. **Hardware Edition**: Focuses on robotics, IoT, renewable energy, electronics, smart vehicles, biomedical devices, etc.
- Each team can submit an idea for **only one Problem Statement (PS)** per edition.
- Multiple teams from the same college can select the same Problem Statement.

## 4. Code of Conduct & Plagiarism
- All code and hardware design developed during the Grand Finale must be original work.
- Existing open-source libraries, packages, and frameworks are allowed, provided proper credit is given.
- Direct plagiarism or buying ready-made projects results in immediate disqualification of the entire team and reporting to the institution.
